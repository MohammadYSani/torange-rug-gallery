MORE THAN A GALLERY

A place to think, meet, and connect.

Coffé Torange is a welcoming space within Torange Rug Gallery created especially for students, researchers, readers, and anyone looking for a comfortable place to spend time.

Settle in with tea or coffee, open a book, work on an idea, meet a friend or colleague, or simply enjoy a quiet moment surrounded by the art and character of handmade rugs.

01

STUDY

Bring your work.

Students and researchers are welcome to bring a laptop, notebook, paper, or book and make themselves comfortable.

02

CONVERSATION

Exchange ideas.

Meet a friend, colleague, or fellow student. Coffé Torange is a place for conversation, discussion, and the unexpected ideas that can emerge when people spend time together.

03

REFRESH

Tea, coffee, and a pause.

Enjoy tea, coffee, and light refreshments while you work, talk, browse the gallery, or simply take a break from the day.
